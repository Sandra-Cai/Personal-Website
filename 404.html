A gate from Gauss

Carl Friedrich Gauss gave the ring of Gaussian integers ℤ[i] its modern shape. An odd prime p ∈ ℤ remains prime (inert) in ℤ[i] if and only if p ≡ 3 (mod 4); if p ≡ 1 (mod 4) it splits.

Let S be the set of all primes p satisfying 100 < p < 200 that remain prime in ℤ[i], and let T be the set of all primes q in the same open interval that split in ℤ[i].

  1. Let P be the product of every prime in S that is strictly greater than 150.
  2. Compute P + |T|! (ordinary factorial of the cardinality of T).
  3. Enter that integer below: digits only, no commas or spaces.
